VM compute

Standard 2 shared vCPUs / 4 GB: $0.35/h. Large 2 shared vCPUs / 8 GB: $0.70/h. Per-second opt-in overage after included credit.

Workspace storage

Included in Pro: one retained 10 GB workspace, also while compute is stopped. Additional workspaces/storage are deferred.

Outbound traffic

Model usage

Overage

Disabled by default. Explicit opt-in plus a hard monetary cap; default extra spend $0.
